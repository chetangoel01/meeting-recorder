# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user takes meetings on a Mac through native clients or any web browser. They need a dependable prompt at the moment a call starts because forgetting to begin a recording is the dominant failure. They already use OpenRouter speech-to-text and want the transcript without automated analysis.

## Product Purpose

Meeting Recorder is a local-first macOS utility that notices likely calls, asks before recording, captures both sides, and produces a plain transcript. Success means supported meetings are difficult to miss, recordings survive transient failures, and the user can recover the source audio until transcription succeeds.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, native, dependable.

## Anti-references

- AI-heavy meeting dashboards that lead with summaries, scores, or generated action items.
- Noisy floating assistants that demand attention throughout a call.
- Generic SaaS styling transplanted into a desktop utility.

## Design Principles

- Prefer an occasional extra prompt over a missed meeting.
- Ask before recording and keep recording status unmistakable.
- Put the primary action at the camera notch where the meeting context already lives.
- Preserve audio until a transcript is safely stored.
- Keep advanced configuration out of the recording path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and reduced-transparency fallbacks. Never encode recording or failure state through color alone.
